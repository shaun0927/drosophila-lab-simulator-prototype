# Motivation-focused playtest guide

Each micro-prototype now exposes its **motivation hypothesis** and **judge-this question** in the right-side log and final result screen.

Use this guide while playing the 15 candidates. Do not judge by visual polish. Judge whether the loop creates a real reason to continue.

## Score each candidate 1-5

- **Curiosity:** do I want to reveal what happens next?
- **Agency:** did my choice express a strategy or identity?
- **Competence:** could I get better on a second run?
- **Tension:** did I hesitate before choosing?
- **Payoff:** was the result funny, surprising, or satisfying?
- **Replay desire:** do I want to try a different route?
- **Presentation:** can this be explained in a 30-second pitch?

## Decision rule

A candidate is worth expanding only if it scores at least 4/5 on two of these:

- Curiosity
- Tension
- Payoff
- Replay desire

A candidate should be cut or merged if it only has a good theme but no replay desire.
