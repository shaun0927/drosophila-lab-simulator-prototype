# Ideation Dossier Guide

## Why this was added

Junghwan asked for more than an imagined 5-minute flow. The candidates need to be easier to evaluate and ideate from.

So every idea now includes an **Ideation dossier** with:

- core fantasy
- player verbs
- first screen sketch
- signature moment
- why it might be fun
- playtest watch points
- likely problem
- bespoke prototype requirement
- three variations to try
- final decision question

## How to judge a candidate

Open an idea page and ask:

1. **Core fantasy:** do I want to be this kind of player?
2. **Player verbs:** are the actual actions interesting, not just the theme?
3. **First screen:** can I imagine a clear screenshot?
4. **Signature moment:** is there a trailer/share moment?
5. **Why fun:** does the motive map to a real game behavior?
6. **Likely problem:** is the risk fatal or fixable?
7. **Bespoke requirement:** can this be prototyped in 1-2 days?
8. **Variations:** does the idea branch into scientific, absurd, or systemic versions?

## How to use it for ideation

When an idea seems close but not right, use the three variations:

- More scientific
- More absurd
- More systemic

This should produce second-generation candidates without returning to raw brainstorming.

## Cut rule

Cut an idea if:

- the first screen is not imaginable
- the signature moment is weak
- the player verbs are passive
- it only works as a joke title
- it does not need Drosophila/neuroscience/research context
